Tone: concrete, calm, evidence-led. Product is BuyerSwitch; analytical module is Switch Radar. Retain the supplied Stagwell branding as a design input; public portfolio naming remains subject to the product owner's approval.

## Marketing

Headline: **See where competitors win on the things buyers talk about.**

Subhead: **Compare product strengths and complaints, inspect the evidence, and decide what to investigate next. Explore the BuyerSwitch concept with a synthetic audio-category dataset.**

Primary CTA: **Explore demo** → /demo/ready.
Secondary: **Preview setup** → /signup.
Badge: **Interactive concept · Synthetic data**.

Value props:
- Attribute-level comparisons: See how product opinions differ across the selected category.
- Evidence beside every finding: Read the full illustrative review behind a highlighted opinion.
- A next step for your team: Save a competitive question and export a brief with its supporting data.

Product limitation: **Reviews describe what reviewers experienced. They do not establish why non-buyers chose a competitor or measure market share, switching rates or causal sales impact.**

No logo wall, customer testimonials, “most popular” badge or claims of universal coverage. Describe live collection, daily refresh and future decision evidence as proposed capabilities.
